# Dental-Implant-Patient-Outcome-Analysis

Dental implant data: The hardness of metal implants in dental cavities depends on multiple factors, such as the method of implant, the temperature at which the metal is treated, the alloy used as well as the dentists who may favor one method above another and may work better in his/her favorite method. The response is the variable of interest.

Background

A dental implant is a prosthesis that interfaces with the bone of the jaw or skull to support a dental prosthesis or act as an orthodontic anchor. Different alloys are being used in the making of the dental implant, which depends on a number of factors such as method of implant, the temperature at which the metal is treated etc.

Objective

* To derive conclusions regarding the hardness of the implants comprising different alloys.
* To check how different variables contribute to the hardness of the implants.
* To check the interaction between the variables.

Conclusion

* The F statistic, that is, the variance between dentist and method interaction is about 1.9 times within each dentist and method interaction.
* The p-value (0.09) is higher than the level of significance, which implies that we fail to reject null hypothesis. 
* There is no evident intersection between all the 3 methods which questions the potential interaction between the dentist and the methods used. 
* When dentist 1 employs method 2, the mean response is higher, whereas it drops when method 3 is used.
* When dentist 5 employs method 1, the mean response is higher, whereas it drops when method 3 is used.
* Method 1 shows a higher response with dentist 2, whereas method 3 demonstrates a decrease in response.
* Method 3 shows significantly reduced response when used by dentist 4. 
* The lines representing method 1 and method 2 interact with each other however the method 3 does not.
* At dentist 3, all three methods yield similar responses, indicating there might not be substantial interaction among the methods linked to dentist 3.
* We have enough statistical evidence to conclude that there is no interaction effect between dentist and method on the hardness of implants comprising alloy 2.
